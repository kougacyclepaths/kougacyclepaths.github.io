Club Rides

What Our Rides Are About

We welcome cyclists of all levels. Here's what to expect when you join us on the road.

🗓️

Regular Rides

We organise regular group rides through the scenic Kouga region. Rides typically take place on weekends, with distances ranging from casual 30km social rides to more challenging 80km+ routes.

🤝

Ride Etiquette

We ride as a group and look out for each other. Helmets are mandatory — call out hazards, signal turns, and no one gets left behind. Ride within your limits and communicate with your fellow riders.

⚡

Pace Groups

Rides are organised by pace so everyone can participate comfortably. Whether you're a casual rider or a seasoned cyclist, there's a group for you. Bikes of all types are welcome.

📍

Meeting Point

Ride details — including meeting point, time, and route — are shared via our Strava club before each ride. Join the club on Strava to stay up to date.
